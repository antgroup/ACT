# ACT 2.1 finalization decision

> Status: Accepted · 2026-08-11

ACT 2.1 ADD, CID, PSD and TSD semantics are final and normative. The scenario guide is informative. A402 schemas, fixtures and assertions under `code/schemas/` are non-normative implementation artifacts. Product integrations and demos do not define ACT semantics or prove conformance.

The versioned release files under `docs/specification/` are the ACT 2.1 publication text. [act-protocol.com](https://www.act-protocol.com/) is the public protocol entry. Interoperability and production claims require separate evidence.
